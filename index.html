How to use this series

  1. Work through one session a week. Each session is one lecture by Clowney (about an hour) and two shorter sermons that preach the same passage or theme.
  2. Read the passage under the session title before you listen.
  3. Hear the lecture first. Then hear the sermons and ask how each preacher moves from the text to Christ.

Press any play button to start. The player at the bottom keeps your place, and “Mark as heard” is saved in this browser only. Speed control is in the player. If a recording will not play here, use “Open in new tab” to stream it straight from the archive.
